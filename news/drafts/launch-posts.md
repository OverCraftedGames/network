# OCG+ is now a monthly loot drop

OCG+ has been rebuilt around loot. Pick one of three tiers and get a drop every month, on every OCG server:

- **Charged**: $5 a month
- **Overcharged**: $10 a month
- **Supercharged**: $20 a month

Every drop includes Credits and a crate key, plus a bolt by your name in chat. Higher tiers get bigger keys, more Credits and double vote keys, and Supercharged adds /fly in your claims. See exactly what each tier gets at <https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz/plus.html>

Perks like extra homes and commands now come from **network ranks**. Ranks are permanent, work on every server, and stack with OCG+.

You can cancel any time from your Tebex payment history, and anything you've already received stays yours.

# Welcome to OCG Network

CarbonCraft is now **OCG Survival**, the first server on **OCG Network**: one home for every Minecraft server we run at OverCrafted Games.

Your world, items, claims, money and friends all stay right where they are.

**What's new**
- **New address:** `play.ocgames.xyz` (`play.carboncraft.xyz` still works)
- **One store for every server:** <https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz>
- **Network ranks:** buy once, keep it for good, use it on every OCG server.
- **OCG+:** a monthly loot drop of Credits and crate keys, on every server.
- **Credits:** the network currency. One balance you can spend anywhere.

**Coming from CarbonCraft?**
- **CarbonCoins become Credits.** Your balance carries over 1:1, automatically. Check it with `/credits` and see every purchase with `/credits history`. The coin packs stay the same, at the same prices. Need to move Credits to a friend? Ask staff and they'll set it up.
- **Carbon+ (1, 3 or 6 months)** keeps every perk until the time you paid for runs out. It won't turn into OCG+ on its own, so pick an OCG+ tier if you want to carry on.
- **Carbon+ Lifetime** holders get our **top network rank**, free and permanent, on every server. We'll add it for you on switch-over day.
- **Crate keys** don't change. Your keys and crates keep the same names.

We'll post the switch-over date here before anything changes in game. Until then, the current store and CarbonCoins work as normal.

**What's next**
A network lobby and **OCG Skyblock** are on the way. More soon.

Something look wrong after the switch? Ask us here in Discord and we'll sort it.
