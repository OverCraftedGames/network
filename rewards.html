Store rewards

Spend more. Get more.

On OCG Survival, everything you buy also counts towards rewards on top of the package itself: monthly bonuses, a server goal that pays out to everyone who plays, and Supporter of the Month.

Monthly rewards, the server goal and Supporter of the Month start in November 2026. Months run on UTC time, from the 1st to the last day.

Buy anything

Every purchase from the store counts, whatever the package.

It adds up

Your total for the month unlocks rewards, and every purchase also moves the server goal.

Claim in game

Rewards wait for you in /storerewards for as long as you like.

Server goal

Everyone wins together

Every purchase from every player counts towards a server-wide goal for the month. When it reaches a stage, everyone who has played 3 hours that month gets the reward, whether they bought anything or not.

Claiming

Your rewards chest

Type /storerewards (or /claimrewards) in game. Click a reward to claim it, or Claim All to take everything that fits.

Credits go straight into your balance and need no space. Plushies need free inventory slots, and each reward shows how many. You'll get a reminder in chat when you join with something unclaimed.

Good to know

Give it a few minutes

Your store package arrives instantly, but these extra rewards are checked every five minutes. If one hasn't shown up yet, give it a moment.

Your head at the top of /storerewards shows your lifetime support, this month's total and your next monthly reward.
